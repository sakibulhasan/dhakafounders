1. Color Palette
Primary Blue: #2A81C7 (A trustworthy, energetic tech blue used in the hero gradient, buttons, and stat highlights).

Secondary Dark Slate: #1F2532 (A deep, professional navy/slate used for the "Our Services" background and the footer, providing high contrast).

Background/Surface: #FFFFFF (Pure white for card backgrounds and the "About" section to maintain a clean layout).

2. Google Fonts Recommendations
Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans (or Poppins). These provide the modern, geometric, and approachable aesthetic seen in the main "Dhaka Founders" and "Our Services" headers.

Body: Inter. Highly legible at smaller sizes and the gold standard for modern SaaS dashboards, matching the clean paragraph text used in the service cards.

3. Tone of Voice
Community-Driven & Supportive: The copy relies heavily on inclusive, collaborative language ("grow and fund their dreams together," "support each other's growth," "vibrant community").

Action-Oriented & Pragmatic: While inspiring, it focuses on tangible utility for software builders and entrepreneurs, highlighting "funding support," "founder resources," and direct connections to investors.
